Key Learnings:

1. Only install extensions from trusted developers.
2. Avoid redundant or unnecessary extensions.
3. Malicious extensions can steal data, track browsing, inject ads, or install malware.
4. Regular audits improve both security and performance.

How Malicious Extensions Cause Harm:

1. Data Theft: These extensions can log your keystrokes to steal passwords and other sensitive information, intercept data you submit on websites, and steal session cookies to impersonate you on various sites.
2. Malware Installation: They can secretly install other malware or spyware on your system, which then monitors your activity or exfiltrates data in the background without your knowledge.
3. Browser Hijacking: Malicious extensions can change your browser's settings, redirecting you to dangerous or fraudulent websites to deceive you into divulging information or clicking on malicious links.
4. Surveillance and Monitoring: Some extensions can spy on your browsing history, take screenshots of your screen, and monitor your web traffic without consent.
5. Account Compromise: By stealing authentication tokens or session cookies, attackers can gain unauthorized access to your online accounts, including social media and banking platforms.
6. Cryptocurrency Theft: Malicious extensions can target users with cryptocurrency wallets, making it easier for attackers to steal funds.
